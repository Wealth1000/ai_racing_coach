# App Logo Vision — AI Sim Racing Coach

A design brief for the coach's app icon and wordmark. The logo should read at
16 px (browser tab, taskbar) and still look sharp on a Steam-style library
tile.

## The idea in one sentence

A stylized corner apex — the racing line tightening through a turn — drawn as
a live telemetry trace: the moment the whole app exists for, the corner you
are about to drive better.

## Concept

- The core mark is **a single bold curve turning through an apex**, drawn
  like a data trace: it enters fast and loose on the left, tightens
  geometrically at the apex (the thickest, brightest point), and exits
  clean and level on the right.
- At the apex sits a **braking marker** — the small numbered boards real
  tracks use before corners (100 / 50 / board) — reduced to one clean
  geometric slash. It says "the coach knows where to brake" without any
  words.
- Optional secondary element: two or three faint **speed lines** behind the
  curve, or a subtle grid the trace is plotted against, hinting at the
  1-metre distance grid the analysis runs on.

## Palette

Dark, motorsport, data. Background is a deep graphite; the trace is the hero.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | deep graphite, near-black blue | `#161A1F` |
| Racing line / hero | signal amber (braking lights, marshal boards) | `#EEBB33` |
| Apex accent | warm red (the moment of maximum attack) | `#E74C3C` |
| Secondary / grid | steel blue-grey (telemetry, instruments) | `#8899AA` |
| Optional highlight | live green (the "● live" connection dot) | `#2ECC71` |

The amber line on graphite is the primary pairing; red only at the apex
point. No gradients that fade to white — this sits on dark dashboards.

## Style

- **Flat, geometric, high contrast.** One weight of curve, generous negative
  space. Think motorsport livery meets a modern charting library.
- The trace should feel *drawn by data, not by hand*: slightly technical,
  rounded line caps, no sketch texture.
- No chrome, no 3D bevels, no drop shadows on the mark itself.
- Must survive monochrome (single-colour trace on any background).

## Composition

- **Icon-only variant (square):** the apex curve diagonal from lower-left to
  upper-right, marker slash at the tightest point, filling ~70% of the tile.
  Works as an app icon and a favicon.
- **Wordmark variant (horizontal):** icon at left, "AI RACING COACH" in a
  condensed, technical sans (something in the family of DIN / Barlow
  Condensed / Rajdhani — instrument-panel typography), all caps, wide
  tracking, steel blue-grey. "RACING" can carry the amber.

## What to avoid

- No cars, helmets, steering wheels, or checkered flags — every racing app
  does that; this one is about the *data of driving*, not the props.
- No text inside the icon-only variant (it must read at 16 px).
- No red/green together on the mark (colour-blind drivers glance at this at
  200 km/h).
- Nothing that implies cloud/online — the app is proudly offline.

## Mood words for the generator

`motorsport telemetry, racing line apex, data trace, braking marker, amber on
graphite, flat geometric, instrument panel, condensed technical, high
contrast, minimal`
